Scope: whole LMS portal app shell and all routes. Mode: Operate.

Audience/job: students track and do coursework; teachers review enrollment and progress. Demo goal: win a learning-center contract.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A working LMS that reads as calm and summery, not a generic admin template. Pinned by the brief (reference-screenshot layout + client palette), so no concept roll.

OWN-WORLD: White cards on a faint cool-mint page, 16px radii, soft teal-tinted shadows. Deep teal fills carry the shell's weight (active pill, hero, stat cards, buttons, headings). Mint for soft surfaces and pills. Lemon only as accent (quiz icons, completed checks, top-scorer badges, focus rings) with ochre for text on light. Nunito Sans; bold teal headings; thin lucide icons.

STORY: Student sees where they are in every course and continues. Teacher sees enrollment health at a glance and acts on applicants.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left sidebar (seal + wordmark, "Menus", teal pill active item, user footer). Top bar: collapse left, Student/Teacher toggle + avatar right. Student: gradient welcome banner then 4-col course grid. Teacher: header, three teal stat cards, tabbed matrix.

FORM: pinned by brief, no seed key (concept-seed skipped: brief-pinned direction). Signature move: the rotating seal on first load settling upright.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
